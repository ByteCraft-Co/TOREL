# Community Conduct

TOREL project spaces are for technical collaboration. Discussion should remain constructive, evidence-oriented, and respectful.

## Expected conduct

Participants should:

- critique ideas, designs, code, and evidence rather than attacking people;
- explain technical disagreement clearly and in good faith;
- respect privacy and confidentiality;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, and personal abuse;
- avoid deliberate disruption, spam, or repeated bad-faith argument;
- give contributors room to correct mistakes;
- keep reviews focused on the work.

Strong technical criticism is welcome. Personal hostility is not.

## Maintainer action

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that materially violate these expectations.

Serious or repeated violations may lead to temporary or permanent exclusion from official TOREL project spaces.

## Scope

This policy applies to official TOREL repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-managed collaboration spaces.
